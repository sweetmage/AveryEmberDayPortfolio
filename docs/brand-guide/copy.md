## Logo

**Final Design:** The bubble logo is a cluster of soft blue bubbles, with the initials A E D hand-lettered
in the lowest one. It comes in two versions: the Primary logo, with the initials, and the Icon Mark, the
bubbles alone. Each comes in blue, black and white.

**Clear Space:** Give the logo room to breathe. Keep a clear space around it, free of text, images and
other graphics, so it stays easy to read. The unit of measure is the width of the small bubble on the left, which is 30% of the logo's full width. Each version has its own spacing, shown below.


**Sizes**

- Primary logo: Use the Primary logo wherever there is room for the initials to read clearly: website
  headers, presentations, print and other full-size pieces. The smallest size is 64px; below that the AED lettering stops reading.
- Icon Mark: Use the Icon Mark in small spaces where the initials would blur: profile pictures,
  favicons and app icons. The smallest size is 24px; below that the bubbles begin to merge.

**Color Variations**

- Blue logo: The everyday logo for digital and print, where the brand's personality should come
  through: web headers, social media and marketing pieces. Place it on dark backgrounds.
- White logo: Sits on dark backgrounds: black, deep gray or any dark color with strong contrast.
- Black logo: Sits on light backgrounds, such as white, cream or light gray, and is the choice for
  one-color printing.

*Check: the blue logo is placed on dark here, matching the site's swatches.*

**Logo Usage**

Do:

- Use only the logo versions in this guide.
- Keep the clear space around the logo.
- Place the logo on backgrounds with strong contrast.
- Scale the logo evenly, keeping its shape.
- Use the white logo on dark backgrounds and the black logo on light ones.

Keep it intact:

- Keep the logo's proportions.
- Keep the logo's own colors, with no gradients or effects added.
- Keep the bubbles and lettering where they are drawn.
- Choose calm, high-contrast backgrounds; busy photos and patterns hide the bubbles.
- Keep the logo flat, without shadows, outlines or filters.
- Keep every bubble; cropped or missing bubbles break the mark.
- Use the supplied files every time; a redrawn logo drifts.

*A plain "Do not" list is standard in logo guides. If you prefer that heading, say so.*

## Color

The palette is soft and bright: Brand Blue leads, with Accent purple, Neon and Gold for pops, and the
iridescent spectrum for shimmer. Colors are listed in their dark-theme values, the brand's home base.
The logo blue (#7EB8FF) is the logo's own color; Brand Blue (#9ACDFF) is the site's.

Print note: Neon and Accent are brighter than ink can print, so their CMYK values are the closest match.

## Typography

Three typefaces, each with its own job, keep every page clear and consistent.

- **Sriracha**, display: big, friendly moments like the brand name and page titles. Short words, large
  sizes.
- **Outfit**, headings: section titles and labels, usually SemiBold (600). Light to Bold (300 to 700)
  are all available.
- **Inter**, body: paragraphs, captions and everything people read at length, usually Regular (400).
  Light to SemiBold (300 to 600) are all available.

## Footnote: previous retro brand

*This section is the only place the retro brand appears. It records the 2025 college guide's brand,
which the bubble brand replaces. Nothing in the main guide refers back to it.*

**The retro logo (previous version):** A coral circle and a teal triangle around "Avery Ember" in a pink
script and "DAY" in caps, with an AED monogram and a star icon for small spaces. It carried the brand
through its first year.

**Evolution of the retro mark**

First iteration (previous draft wording: "This logo was my first personal brand mark, developed using the
same moodboard that continues to guide my design direction. I was drawn to the triangle for its balance
and clarity, and modified it by removing the top-left corner to create a simple, scalable icon.")

- The first personal brand mark: a triangle, chosen for its balance and clarity, with the top-left
  corner removed to make a simple icon that scales well.

Second iteration (previous draft wording: "Building on the original design, I reworked the triangle into
a right-angled form and placed it within a circle to create a more structured, balanced mark. The
geometric contrast added visual strength while improving legibility across various sizes. This version
reinforced the identity's bold, modern character and helped solidify the direction for my final logo.")

- The triangle became a right-angled form inside a circle, a steadier, more balanced mark. The contrast
  of the two shapes made it stronger and easier to read at every size, and set the direction for the
  retro logo.

**The bubble logo (current):** The bubble logo trades hard geometry for soft, round shapes and a single
calm blue. It keeps the AED initials and the idea of a full mark and a small icon, and feels friendlier
and lighter at every size.
