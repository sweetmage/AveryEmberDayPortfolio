## Acceptance contract

- All 12 gallery items carry a `mockup` (src, width, height, alt, kind). Gross is `kind:
  'skateboard'`; the other 11 use a wall scene (`canvas`, `poster` or `framed`).
- Mockup images live under `public/images/myart/Mockups/`, generated by a committed, re-runnable
  script from committed scene sources under `images/mockups/scenes/` and a manifest.
- Each mockup's art region is the artwork's own pixels, scaled, at its own aspect ratio (wall
  scenes), or cover-cropped into the deck shape (skateboard). Never distorted.
- The mockup renders **only when the card is expanded**, after the description slot, as its own
  `<img>` with real alt text, lazy loaded, with srcset variants. Collapsed cards are byte-identical
  in layout, so the 40 visual baselines do not move.
- The `mockup` `<img>` is a bubble wall automatically (`img` is in `DEFAULT_EXCLUSIONS`); nothing
  is renamed out of that list.
- Tests: a new spec proves hidden-when-collapsed, shown-when-expanded, alt present, Gross is the
  skateboard, every mockup file exists and matches its declared dimensions. The existing suite stays
  green.

**Scene set (Track A):**

1. `wall-living` - front-facing, eye-level empty light wall above a low sofa or console; large
   clear area in the upper middle. Used for `canvas` (art with a drawn canvas edge and soft drop
   shadow).
2. `wall-studio` - front-facing empty concrete or plaster wall in a studio or hallway. Used for
   `framed` (thin black frame plus white mat, drawn in code).
3. `wall-bedroom` - front-facing empty warm-toned wall above a bed or desk. Used for `poster`
   (paper with a slight curl shadow and tape or clips, drawn in code).
4. `skateboard-underside` - a skateboard deck seen from below, face-on, blank pure white graphic
   area, trucks and wheels visible, on a neutral background. Deck mask is found from the white
   region; Gross is cover-cropped into it and multiplied by the scene's own shading.

Front-facing walls need no perspective warp: the canvas, frame or poster is drawn in code at the
art's own aspect ratio, which is what keeps every piece undistorted whatever its shape (aspect
0.61 to 1.5 across the 12).

## Open risks

- **Generated scene quality.** A scene with visible text, a stray frame on the wall, or a warped
  deck gets regenerated (up to 3 tries per scene), then falls back to a code-drawn scene for that
  slot and is recorded here.
- **Skateboard crop.** A deck is about 1:3.8; Gross is about 0.81. Cover-cropping shows a vertical
  band of the piece. Focal point is configurable in the manifest; the main agent picks it by eye.
- **Page weight.** 12 extra images, lazy and only fetched on expand. Budget ~150 KB each at 1600px
  webp; variants cover mobile.
- **Card height.** The expanded card grows by the mockup's height. The art is already capped at one
  screen; the mockup is capped the same way so an expanded card is at most about two screens.
