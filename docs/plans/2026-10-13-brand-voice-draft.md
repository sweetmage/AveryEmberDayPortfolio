## Brand Voice and Tone

I sound like a collaborator who listens first, says things plainly, and keeps a little wonder in the corner of the page. After reading, you should feel heard, never sold to.

### Plainspoken, not stiff
I say what it is first, then one detail. Short sentences, and a verb instead of an abstract noun.
*"A 30-slide Instagram series on why some communities struggle to trust doctors."*

### Warm, and I listen first, not salesy
I invite once, and I talk to you, not at you. Every project starts with listening.
*"Tell me what you're working on and I'll get back to you as soon as I can."*

### Dry, with a little wonder, not cute
One wink a page, at most, and none on heavy subjects.
*"I woke up in a world that was falling apart at the seams, with a crayon in my hand."*

### Tone changes, the voice does not

| Where | How it sounds |
|---|---|
| About | Warm and personal, one wink allowed |
| Project pages | Matter-of-fact and informative |
| Heavy subjects, like A History of Mistrust | Sober, factual, humane, no wink |
| Contact | Friendly, specific, short |
| Gallery titles | Blunt, a little playful |
| Alt text | Literal and plain |

### Words I use
Systems, reusable, built to last, character, mood, craft, clean, clear, accessible, listening, the brief.

### Words I skip
| Skip | Say instead |
|---|---|
| passionate | the thing I actually do |
| creative solutions, unique, stunning, seamless | what it does |
| leverage, utilize | use |
| "feel free to reach out" | "reach out" |
| "we" | "I" |

### How I write
First person. Contractions, yes. No em dashes. At most one exclamation mark a page. US spelling. One title: Brand & Visual Designer.
