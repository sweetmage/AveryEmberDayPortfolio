Patriots Low Thirds

Broadcast lower thirds package for the New England Patriots from motion brief and storyboard to final After Effects render. Designed for high readability at 1080p with team brand colors and kinetic typography.

Brief

Create a dynamic lower thirds system for a Patriots broadcast segment. The design needed to feel fast, athletic, and on brand while keeping player names and stats legible against live game footage. I explored several kinetic type directions before landing on a split slide reveal with a chrome bevel and subtle motion blur.
